## The finding (the scope)

While the phase is `changing`, session-start.py's PHASE_MODULES names change-cycle, delegation-loop, reflection and project-end, and collect_conditional_files names only conditionals touching those. After a compaction in a change cycle's intake, design or interface step, the conditionals touching that step's module were therefore never named: claude-code-extension.md at design, local-service.md at the interface, local-web-app.md and own-computer.md at intake. The fix adds "with the conditional workflows the `Workflows:` line names that touch that module, each whole" and a parenthesis naming what the hook does name.
