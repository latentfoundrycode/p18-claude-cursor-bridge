# TASK-084: A merge unit of one increment is refused with its cause (R-105)

## Objective
A brief declares a merge unit only where its branch builds two or more increments; a brief of one increment declares no unit at all. `bridge/cursor-bridge/bridge-run.py` (the launcher) reads a brief's unit through `bridge_env.py`, whose prose pattern `MERGE_UNIT_RE` matches a `Merge unit:` line of two or more IDs only, while the header's reader `_merge_unit_from_header` takes a header list of one ID as a unit. So a brief written for one increment with a `Merge unit:` line naming that one ID meets three answers today, none of which names the cause: beside a header listing the ID (the field's case, finding F7) it is refused with `header: disagrees merge_unit`; beside `merge_unit: null`, or in a brief with no header, it is launched with the line unread; and a header listing one ID with no line is refused with `header: disagrees merge_unit`. This increment adds one brief rule to the launcher that refuses, before any run and with exit 125, a brief that names a merge unit of one increment and no unit of two or more, with a line of its own that says what a merge unit is, what a brief of one increment carries, and which side of the brief named the one. A merge unit of one is not made valid, and no reader of a brief changes what it returns. The supervisor's acceptance tests are written and committed; your work is to make them pass without breaking the existing ones.

Satisfies: R-105 - A brief that names a merge unit of one increment is refused with its cause (F7); its full text, from `docs/REQUIREMENTS.md`, follows in the next paragraph.

The requirement's text as the register holds it: "A brief that names a merge unit of one increment is refused with its cause (F7): before any run, with exit 125, the launcher refuses a brief whose `Merge unit:` line holds exactly one word of the pattern's ID form (a capital letter, then letters, digits and hyphens), or whose header's `merge_unit` lists exactly one, where neither the prose nor the header names two or more as written (a `Merge unit:` line with two or more words after its colon, a list of two or more entries), by a brief-rule line saying that a merge unit names two or more increments and that a brief of one increment carries no such line and `merge_unit: null`, and naming the side that named the one, the line, the header or both, where 2026.11.12a answered `header: disagrees merge_unit` for a header listing the one ID and launched a line of one ID beside `merge_unit: null` with the line unread; the rule stands after the header's refusals for a header that cannot be read or that breaks its schema and for a disagreement of `id`, `round`, `satisfies` or `scope`, and before those of `merge_unit` and `blocking_class`; a line and a header of which one names two or more keep `header: disagrees merge_unit`, and in the cases the rule answers its line stands in place of the launcher's refusal as R-059 states it, the review guard's standing as built, R-059's row gaining the clause when the rule is built; no reader of a brief returns anything it did not return before and the pattern's text is unchanged, so no record reads differently; `spec-packager` says that a merge unit is two or more increments and that a brief of one increment has no `Merge unit:` line and `merge_unit: null` in its header." Of that text this increment builds the launcher's rule; the `spec-packager` sentence is a later increment's (TASK-082), and R-059's clause is the supervisor's records commit.
