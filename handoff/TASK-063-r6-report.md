## Summary

Round 6 keeps the flat reader and fixes the local defects in it: empty quotes stay words, a backslash eats only one newline, redirection targets are the next word of the same loop, braces and named descriptors are read character by character, one prefix walk serves refusals, `strip_prefix` and `HUSKY=0`, `.exe` is stripped only for shell and launcher names, the builtin and cmd and PowerShell tables match the brief, rule 8 sees every pipeline stage, and a glued commit message is the checkpoint subject.

## Requirements

1. `_read_bash` and `_read_powershell` set `word_started` when a quote opens, keep an empty quoted bash word, refuse an empty PowerShell word, and start a `#` comment only when no word has started.
2. `_read_bash` (the unquoted and double-quoted backslash arms) removes a backslash and exactly one newline, and stores a backslash-escaped carriage return in the word.
3. Redirection targets are the next word completed in the same loop by `_read_bash`, `_read_powershell` and `_read_cmd` through `pending`, and no second scanner of redirection targets remains.
4. `_read_bash` `start_redirect` refuses a word that is wholly `{name}` when the next character is `<` or `>`.
5. `_walk_to_command` is the one prefix walk used by `_cmd_word_refusal`, `strip_prefix` and `leading_husky_zero`.
6. `_exe_stem` strips one trailing `.exe` before shell, wrapper, prefix, launcher and builtin comparisons, and `around_the_shim` still compares `cursor-agent.exe` unchanged.
7. `REFUSED_BUILTINS` is the round 5 builtin list minus the allowed builtins and the wrappers, including `compgen`, `complete`, `compopt` and `continue`, and `_bash_reads_stdin` treats `-`, `/dev/` and `/proc/` as a shell that reads a descriptor.
8. The readers `_read_bash`, `_read_powershell` and `_read_cmd` contain no `text[i:]` slice, no regular expression at a character, and no split of the rest of the text.
9. `_read_bash` refuses `$` and a backtick outside single quotes and comments whether or not a backslash stands before them.
10. `_parse_cmd_wrapper` reads every switch before the inner command, and `_read_cmd` splits on `&`, `&&`, `||` and `|` and takes `<`, `>` and `>>` targets out.
11. `_read_powershell` refuses a character outside printable ASCII via `_ps_char_ok`, reads descriptors `1`–`6` and `*` and completes `n>&1` and `n>&2` with no target, refuses a module-qualified `Verb-Noun` or alias tail, and `_parse_ps_wrapper` no longer treats `-noexit` as a known switch.
12. `_stage_changes` marks a part state-changing when any of its stages is, and `chain_reason` applies the `cd` rule only when `outermost` is true, which `_check_one` sets from `depth == 0`.
12a. `leading_husky_zero` calls `_walk_to_command`, so `HUSKY=0` after prefix options is seen.
13. `commit_message` reads `-m<text>` and `--message=<text>` as well as `-m <text>`.
14. The docstring sentence is now "A here-document is unreadable." and `python tools/dump-cli.py` regenerated the command reference.
15. `_snapshot`, `READ_CACHE` and `raw_parts` are deleted.
16. The new cases live in `tests/test_permission_guard_a4.py` and go through `_run_r6`, whose `subprocess.run` passes `creationflags=getattr(subprocess, "CREATE_NO_WINDOW", 0)` and a timeout on one line.
